# Privacy

**TMRs Ctrl Msg collects nothing.**

- There is no server, no account, no login, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking code.
- Everything you enter or create (name, designation, phone, CMS ID, signature, contacts, important numbers, saved messages, reports, photos, videos, audio notes, records and settings) is stored **only in your own phone's browser storage**.
- The app makes no network calls after the first load, other than the browser checking for a newer version of the app files when you are online. Fonts are bundled.
- Voice input (the microphone buttons) uses the phone's own voice-typing service, which needs internet and is run by the phone/browser maker, not by this app. Typing never needs it.
- Data leaves your phone only when **you** press a Send / Share / Copy / Download button, and then it goes only where you choose (WhatsApp, SMS, the Share sheet, a file).
- Clearing the browser data, or uninstalling the app, **deletes** everything. Use **Backup now** in My profile / Station codes, contacts & backup to keep a copy, and Restore on a new phone.
- Keep your phone locked and your backup file safe; anyone who can open your phone can open the app.

*Not an official Indian Railways tool.*
